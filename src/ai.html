A 5-step AI adoption plan for small teams

Step 1

List repeatable tasks

Write down tasks done weekly that follow a pattern: drafting replies, summarising calls, tagging tickets, first-draft content.

Step 2

Measure the baseline

Time each task for one week. Without a baseline you cannot prove savings later.

Step 3

Pilot one task

Pick the task with the most hours and the lowest risk if the output is imperfect. Run it with AI for two weeks.

Step 4

Count review time

AI output needs checking. Subtract review minutes from time saved — this is where most ROI estimates go wrong.

Step 5

Scale or stop

If net savings beat the tool cost by 3× or more, roll out. If not, try a different task rather than a bigger tool.
