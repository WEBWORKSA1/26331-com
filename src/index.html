Frequently asked questions

Are the calculators really free?

Yes. Every tool is free with no account. The site is funded by advertising, sponsorships and voluntary donations.

Do you store the numbers I enter?

No. Calculations run entirely in your browser. Only forms you choose to submit (like the growth review) are sent to us.

Is this financial advice?

No. The tools are educational estimates. For decisions with real consequences, speak with a licensed professional — our free growth review can point you to one.

What does “26331” mean?

It’s our domain name. The number itself is 3 × 67 × 131 — we use it as a memorable home for numbers-first tools. See the Number Explorer for more.

Can I advertise, sponsor or partner with you?

Yes — see sponsorship packages or use the inquiry link at the top of every page.
