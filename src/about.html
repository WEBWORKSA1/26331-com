About 26331.com

26331.com — Numbers Lab — is an independent website with one mission: help people make better money, business and AI decisions by running the numbers first.

What we publish

We build free calculators that run in your browser and write short guides that explain the math behind them with worked examples. Our structure is simple and easy to remember: 26 tools, 3 hubs and a 31-day growth plan.

How we work

  • Standard formulas. Every calculator uses widely published formulas, documented in the matching guide.
  • Privacy by design. Calculations happen on your device. We only receive what you choose to submit through a form.
  • Clear labels. Advertising and sponsorships are always labelled. Sponsors never edit our guides or tools.
  • Corrections. Spot an error? Tell us through the contact form and we will fix it and note the update.

How the site is funded

We are funded by display advertising from Google AdSense, clearly labelled sponsorships, referral fees from specialists introduced through our free growth review (only when a visitor chooses to hire them), and voluntary reader donations.

About the name

26331 is our domain name. We use it purely as a memorable numeric address and have no connection to any organisation, product or postal area that uses the same digits.
