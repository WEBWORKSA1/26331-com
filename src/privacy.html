Privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains what information 26331.com (“we”, “us”) collects when you use this website, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you submit a form — contact, growth review, contest entry, talent application, sponsorship inquiry, donation pledge, newsletter or video submission — we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email address and message. Form submissions are delivered to our inbox through a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit). We use this information only to respond to you, deliver what you requested, run contests and, where you opt in, send occasional updates.

Calculator inputs

Numbers you type into our calculators and the Number Explorer are processed in your browser and are not sent to us.

Cookies and local storage

We store small preferences in your browser (for example, that you dismissed the cookie notice or the newsletter pop-up). Third parties described below may also set cookies.

Advertising — Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies at aboutads.info. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message where required, and ads may be non-personalised unless consent is given.

Other third parties

We load fonts from Google Fonts and link to videos on YouTube. These services may receive your IP address and set their own cookies under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep data

We keep form submissions only as long as needed to handle your request, run a contest or meet legal obligations, and generally no longer than 24 months.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Canada’s PIPEDA or California’s CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, use the contact form and choose “Privacy request”. We do not sell personal information.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 where applicable), and contests are open to adults only. We do not knowingly collect children’s information.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the “last updated” date when we do.
