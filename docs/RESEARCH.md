**Why the calculator hub wins**
- **Highest-value ad categories.** Loans, mortgages, credit cards, insurance, business finance and B2B software are consistently among the most expensive ad categories, so finance tools tend to earn well above average RPM. (Planning assumption: $8–$25 RPM on tier-1 finance traffic vs $1–$4 for general content; verify against your own AdSense reports.)
- **Evergreen search demand.** Queries like "loan calculator", "mortgage calculator", "profit margin calculator" and "break-even calculator" have steady, year-round volume.
- **Built-in lead intent.** Someone calculating a loan, runway or AI budget is mid-decision, which makes them a strong lead for funding brokers, web/AI agencies and marketers.
- **Free hosting fit.** All tools run client-side in vanilla JS, so GitHub Pages free tier is enough.
- **The number tools (Number Explorer, cheque writer) use the domain itself** and pull in long-tail number-lookup searches.
